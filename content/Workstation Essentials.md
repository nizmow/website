This is a list of programs I find essential for various computer systems I use, perhaps with some notes of how to set them up.
## Mac
- [Mac Mouse Fix](https://macmousefix.com/) -- use instead of any Logitech bloatware to fix mouse scrolling and to let me bind things to my MX3's extra buttons. Typically this is something to do expose, and hold and drag a side button to move workspaces.
	- Honorable mention: [LinearMouse](https://linearmouse.app/) -- this is open source, and typically I'd prefer that, but I can't find a way to do the hold and drag which I have come to rely on.
- [Wezterm](https://wezterm.org/) -- the best terminal emulator I've found so far. Particularly cool is remote session support... say goodbye to `tmux`.
