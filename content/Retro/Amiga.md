## Software
### Icons and fonts
- [Standardised MagicWB Icons](https://grimore.org/amiga/standardised_magicwb_icons) -- a great pack of MagicWB-style (8 colour) icons. Get them all in one place.
- [Standardised GlowIcons](https://grimore.org/amiga/standardised_glowicons) -- the same but for Amiga GlowIcons, generally 256 colour or thereabouts. Note that these contain the cooler isometric style GlowIcons, not the lame perspective ones that are the default in AmigaOS 3.2.x.
### Workbench packs
Various packs or almost Workbench "distributions" designed for a more modern, friendlier or just fancier out of the box Workbench experience.
Generally I'd prefer to just customise Workbench myself.
* [BestClassicWB](https://classicwb.abime.net/) -- modern yet minimal, inspired by other distributions but without being over the top. One of the few intended to be used with AmigaOS 3.2.x.
* [HstWB Installer](https://hstwb.firstrealize.com/) -- technically not a Workbench pack, as such, but a Windows-based installer tool which will install Workbench to an image, as well as bundle in software or most of the Workbench packs listed here.
* [Classic Workbench](https://classicwb.abime.net/) or "Classic Workbench" -- probably the most well known pack, contains "hires" editions that work well with the classic Amiga PAL "hires" resolution (stretched pixels) as well as those oriented towards squarer screeen modes. A pain to install, requires UAE and some messing about to get it on a real Amiga disk.
* [BestWB](https://classicwb.abime.net/) -- a very light pack that just amends AmigaOS with some updated libraries, commodities and utilities. Suited for AmigaOS 3.1.4, a lot of the stuff included already exists on 3.2.x.
* [BetterWB](https://classicwb.abime.net/) -- from the same folks as BestWB, but oriented towards Commodore AmigaOS (ie: prior to 3.1.4). Includes a bunch of fixes not required for 3.1.4.
* [32se for AmigaOS](https://classicwb.abime.net/) -- I haven't played with this, looks very custom and mostly aimed at either UAE or very high end Amigas with Picasso96.
* [AmiKit](https://classicwb.abime.net/) -- again, super high end stuff. Turns your Amiga into something... more than an Amiga.
